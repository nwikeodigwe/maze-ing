WHAT TO DO RIGHT NOW
Type this, with your own name instead of <name>:

    register <name>

You can't save your game until you've registered, and I really don't
want to be the one who says "just reload" and then watches you lose an
hour of wandering. Save early. Save often. Save before you touch the
mysterious button. There is always a mysterious button.

ON DOING IT "WRONG"
Here's what the in-game help won't tell you: you cannot break this game
by typing the wrong command. Truly. Try `go ceiling`. Try `use
spaghetti on door`. Try `enter 1234`. The worst that happens is the game
shrugs and says it doesn't understand, and you walk away knowing one
more thing that isn't the answer - which is, let's be honest, about 90%
of puzzle solving.

Half of what's hidden in this building is behind the thing you were too
polite to try. The people who built this place built secrets into it
expecting you to be a little unhinged. So be a little unhinged. Think
outside the box. Type the thing nobody else would type.

WHEN YOU'RE READY
`look` around. `go east` back to the lobby. Type `inspect guide` in any
room for the local gossip and `help` for every verb the game knows.

Now go on. The receptionist is watching. Make it weird.
