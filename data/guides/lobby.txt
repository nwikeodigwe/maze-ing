# 🎮 HOW TO PLAY

#    look                  squint at the room you're standingin. 👀
#    go <direction>        head north, south, east, or west (or just go <room>).
#    inspect <thing>       poke it. Read notes. Chat to terminals. 🔍
#    take <item>           pocket it. (drop <item> when the guilt sets in.)
#    inventory             check your pockets. 🎒
#    use <item>            try it out, or use <item> on <thing> to get specific.
#    enter <code>          keypads love this one. 🔢
#    help                  the panic button. 🚨
#    save / load           heroes save often. Be a hero. 💾
