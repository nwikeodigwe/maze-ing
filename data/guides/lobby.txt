Welcome to A-maze-ing Delft!

Let's get the awkward part out of the way first: this is one of those
text adventures where you type things. Commands like `look` and `go east`.
If you're under thirty, think of it as a very slow video game. If you're
over thirty, welcome home.

You're a student at THUAS - The Hague University of Applied Sciences -
and somebody has to build a robot. That somebody is you. Why you? Let's
not dwell on it. Funding ran out, the deadline is basically now, and the
parts are... somewhere in this building. Probably a filing cabinet. Or
under a desk. Buildings are like that.

Your job sounds simple:
1. Wander the building and pocket every part and secret you find.
2. Assemble it all at the workbench in the lab.
3. Get the maze-server in Lab D 2001 configured and running.
   The robot's brain lives there. No server? No robot.
4. Activate the robot and make a new best friend.

HOW TO PLAY
`look`              squint at the room you're standing in.
`go <direction>`    head north, south, east or west (or `go <room>`).
`inspect <thing>`   poke it. Read notes. Chat to terminals.
`take <item>`       pocket it. `drop <item>` when the guilt sets in.
`inventory`         check your pockets.
`use <item>`        try it. `use <item> on <thing>` to be specific.
`enter <code>`      keypads love this one.
`help`              the panic button.
`save` / `load`     heroes save often. Be a hero.

FIRST THINGS FIRST
Go west to the front desk and `register <name>`. The receptionist is
very kind about it. You can't save without registering, and you'll want
to save before doing the thing we both know is coming.

You start here in the lobby. Around you: a classroom, a teachers' room,
and an east corridor that absolutely leads somewhere. Type `inspect
guide` in any room and one of these will explain that room. It's me.
I'm the guide. Hi.

ONE SMALL WARNING
The two labs are called Lab D 2001 and Lab D2 002. Yes, different
formats. Nobody knows why. The whiteboard says "historical reasons",
which is campus-speak for "someone renamed a folder".

Good luck out there. Try not to break the server - and if you do, the
guide in Lab D 2001 will walk you through fixing it. You've got this.
